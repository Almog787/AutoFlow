Frequently Asked Questions

Is the service also suitable for individuals who don't run a business?
Absolutely! We help many individuals bring order to their digital chaos. Whether you're managing a renovation project, a student with tons of materials, or just want an organized system to manage tasks, documents, and budget in one place – we know how to build the perfect infrastructure for it.

Yes. In fact, small businesses and freelancers benefit the most from automation because it allows them to operate like a large organization with a lean team. Saving 5-10 hours a week of administrative work is critical to your growth.

Not at all. We specialize in connecting the tools you already use (like WhatsApp, Google Workspace, accounting software, or calendars) so they "talk" to each other seamlessly and transfer data on their own.
